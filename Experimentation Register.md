# Experimentation Register

Long-term record of research, training, experimentation, certifications, and related outcome-based practice carryover. This file stores detailed progression history; `Ledger Resources.md` stores current resources and `status sheet.md` stores active modifiers.

**Completeness rule:** Record every resolved training or experimentation attempt, including failures, partial outcomes, repeated trials, changed methods, and certifications. Each entry must preserve its Action, Task, margin, result, and next comparable-attempt effect.

## Carryover Rule

For the next directly comparable narrow-method attempt, if the prior margin is below +10:

`Practice Carryover P = max(1, prior margin)`

Apply `+P` to Action and `-P` to Task. A prior +10 or greater result gives no carryover and instead counts as strong competence evidence. Carryover cannot double-count an earned modifier covering the same mastered method.

**Derived Refinement gate:** A related but non-identical technique receives Lineage Familiarity only if each documented predecessor achieved a decisive raw margin of +10 or greater. Use the raw margin before the normal noncombat outcome cap: `L_i = min(3, floor(qualifying predecessor raw margin ÷ 10))`; apply the total of all distinct, non-overlapping `L_i` values as +Action and -Task. A qualifying derived result may become a parent for later work, creating a branching progression tree. A new experiment may have multiple parents when each supplies a distinct necessary component. Every derived entry must name its parent entries, their raw margins, their separate contributions, and their transfer boundaries. Current recorded Day 35–40 experiments have no qualifying +10 predecessor, so none currently grant Lineage Familiarity.
