### Tier 2 Herb Garden

**Tier:** 2

A controlled cultivation site supporting valid Tier 0–2 herb growing, propagation, garden-care experiments, yield planning, and harvest handling. It requires seeds or starter stock, valid care, and normal growth time.

#### Facility Effect

**Cultivation Bonus:** +10 to herb-cultivation, garden-care, yield-planning, and harvest-handling actions performed on site.

Limits: Does not create ingredients from nothing, instantly mature crops, identify unknown herbs, or replace preservation/alchemy work.

#### Capacity and Cultivation Register

**Physical layout:** 12 controlled cultivation beds with **20 standard plant positions each** (**240 standard positions**), plus **24 enclosed propagation positions** for seeds, cuttings, and fragile early growth. A bed can be dedicated to one compatible herb type or divided only when soil, water, mana, spacing, and care requirements remain compatible.

**Tier handling:** The site can hold valid Tier 0–2 living cultivation stock. Capacity is space, not guaranteed production: every plant still requires a viable seed/cutting, appropriate substrate, water, light, care, and normal growth time.

**How the Tier 2 Garden works:** It adds +10 to an actor’s valid on-site herb-cultivation, garden-care, yield-planning, harvest-handling, propagation, and comparable plant-management Action. In addition, a viable living Tier 0–2 plant occupying an active garden position grows at **2× its normal outdoor rate**: one full garden day counts as two normal outdoor growth-days. This growth-rate effect does not create stock, bypass soil/water/light/care requirements, apply to dormant or nonviable material, guarantee higher yield, instantly mature a plant, create a plant-side modifier, or cause a tier increase. Tier advancement, unusual yield, and plant-side effects still require separately resolved valid work.

- structural complexity
- formation time
- active magical construction
- calculation requirements

- Ava successfully interprets the intended structure

- no specific rule forces it to remain a major action

## Resource System

Spell Computer V2 separates spell operation into two different mana costs.

Generating a spell through Spell Computer V2:

- consumes a constant amount of relay-space mana
- uses the Cognitive Relay Space as the processing medium
- pays for interpretation, retrieval, validation, and construction
- does not directly consume the caster’s personal MP during generation

The generation cost is fixed and does not vary based on spell complexity, novelty, or structure.

### 2. Spell Casting Cost

Actually casting the completed spell:

- consumes the caster’s personal MP
- draws from the user’s own mana pool
- follows normal active-skill and spell-cost rules
- may also require SP if physical movement or body strain is involved

### Cost Separation

Spell Computer V2 does not remove resource costs.

It separates them into:

1. Generation Cost: constant relay mana
2. Casting Cost: personal MP and any other required resources

This allows computational burden and casting burden to function independently.

### Efficiency Improvement

Ava and the Knowledge Database reduce unnecessary architectural work.

While generation cost remains constant, efficiency improves reliability, structure quality, and success rate.

## Ava / Analyst Mirror Assistance

**Name:** Ava<br>**Version:** Analyst Mirror V0.2<br>**Type:** Bounded Cognitive Persona Construct / Mental Analysis Assistant<br>**Location:** Cognitive Relay Space / Knowledge Database Rune<br>**Status:** Not alive, not a soul, not a node, not an independent person

### Core Function

Ava is a bounded mental construct capable of analyzing, comparing, organizing, and reasoning through information at approximately human-level analytical capacity within her assigned scope.

She can assist David and the nodes with intellectual tasks by processing stored data, identifying contradictions, checking logic, modeling outcomes, detecting bias, and summarizing complex information.

Ava does not create truth.<br>Ava does not know things that are not available to her.<br>Ava does not override rolls.<br>Ava does not replace skills.<br>Ava does not make impossible actions valid.<br>Ava does not count as an independent NPC or node.

#### Valid Assistance Areas

Ava may assist with:

- analysis
- planning
- research organization
- memory comparison
- contradiction review
- system design
- rune logic
- database work
- strategy review
- argument testing
- bias detection
- report writing
- intellectual problem-solving
- past-life knowledge sorting
- archive safety review

#### Ava Assistance Modifier

When Ava is actively involved in a valid intellectual task and has relevant data to work with, she grants:

**+10% to the final Action Value**

This bonus is applied after normal stats, skills, equipment, situational modifiers, and roll are added together, but before comparing against Task Value.

Example:

Pre-assistance Action Value: 120<br>Ava bonus: 120 × 10% = +12<br>Final Action Value with Ava only: 132

#### Combined Ava and Knowledge Database Assistance

The Knowledge Database is the reference source; Ava is the interpreter and teacher-like guide. They are separate modifiers and may operate together whenever both independently qualify.

- Database only: pre-assistance Action × 1.10.
- Ava only: pre-assistance Action × 1.10.
- Both validly used: Database +10%, Ava +10%, and **+5% retrieval-and-interpretation synergy**, all calculated from the same pre-assistance Action Value: **pre-assistance Action × 1.25**.
- The percentages are additive rather than multiplicative. The combined result is not `1.10 × 1.10`.
- The integrated relay system makes both supports concurrently available, but it does not waive either support’s individual relevance, knowledge, time, or valid-method requirements.

#### Requirements

Ava’s bonus only applies if:

1. The task is primarily intellectual, analytical, structural, strategic, or knowledge-based.
2. Ava is deliberately involved in the action.
3. Ava has access to relevant information.
4. The task gives her enough time to analyze.
5. Her output is actually used in the method.
6. The action is already valid before her bonus is applied.

#### Invalid Uses

Ava’s bonus does not apply to:

- raw physical combat
- direct weapon attacks
- endurance tasks
- stealth movement
- reflex actions
- emotional persuasion by itself
- charisma-only social scenes
- crafting without analysis component
- tasks where Ava lacks relevant data
- tasks where David ignores her output
- invalid actions
- actions requiring a soul, life creation, or higher-tier authority she does not possess

#### Social Use Limit

Ava can help prepare arguments, analyze motives, identify contradictions, and review negotiation logic.

However, she does not directly make people like David more, trust him more, or accept his words.

For social actions, Ava may only assist if the method includes structured reasoning, debate preparation, written argument, policy design, or emotional-pattern analysis.

#### Cost

Minor Ava consultation costs no extra mana beyond her existing maintenance.

Major use may require extra relay mana if she performs heavy modeling, deep archive comparison, multi-branch simulation, or prolonged analysis.

#### Limits

Ava is intelligent as an analytical construct, but her personhood is unproven.

She cannot:

- act outside the Cognitive Relay Space
- spend resources independently
- become a node without future validation
- force conclusions
- serve as a binding judge
- guarantee truth
- solve emotional conflict by logic alone
- bypass tier limits
- remove the need for rolls
