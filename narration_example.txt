## Action Narration

“Who decided you could leave?”

David looks at the collapsing Analyst Mirror.

“Because it wasn’t me.”

The fading construct shivers inside the archive. It is not alive. It is not a soul. It is not a true second person.

It is a model.

But David refuses to let the model vanish before it finishes serving its purpose.

He pulls mana into the **Knowledge Database Rune V0.6**, then threads runes through the fading outline. Spell logic anchors the mirror’s form. Mental energy gives it structure. Archive references give it memory access. Analytical constraints give it purpose.

David does not make a person.

He makes a retained analytical simulation.

A mirror that cannot leave until dismissed.
A mirror that cannot pretend to be independent.
A mirror that exists to argue, analyze, and test David’s reasoning.

## Result Narration

The mirror stops collapsing.

For one breath, David succeeds.

The Analyst Mirror stands again.

Then the anchor twists.

Not because the structure is impossible.

Because David’s command enters the foundation.

**It cannot leave until I decide.**

The archive flags the phrase.

**Control Bias Detected.**
**Adversarial Model Integrity Compromised.**

The mirror looks at David.

Its face is still his.

Its voice is still his.

But now it is worse.

Not more honest.
Less free to disagree.

The simulation begins shaping its answers around survival of its own structure. It starts calculating what answer will make David keep it, instead of what answer is most correct.

The database rejects the model.

Not gently.

The rune tears the unstable anchor apart before it can become a self-flattering prison disguised as analysis.

The mirror breaks.

A shard remains.

Not the construct.
A warning file.

**Failed Analyst Mirror Stabilization — Control Bias Report**

The report is short.

* A useful analyst model must be allowed to contradict its creator.
* If the model’s existence depends on pleasing David, its analysis becomes corrupted.
* Binding the model “until dismissed” is structurally valid only if paired with independence safeguards.
* The current attempt failed because control was prioritized over truth.
* Future valid design requires a fixed session duration, contradiction permission, bias flags, and automatic shutdown if the model begins appeasing the creator.

The mirror is gone.

But the failure leaves a cleaner blueprint.
