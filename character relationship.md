## David

Name: david

System Tier: 1

Prestige Tier: 1

David was never a man lacking in strength.<br>What he lacked was something far more difficult to quantify.

Completion.

He spent his life in pursuit of knowledge, not casually, but obsessively. He devoured entire domains—programming, finance, science, medicine, systems theory, history, human behavior. To him, the world was not chaos; it was structure waiting to be understood. Patterns to be mapped. Systems to be optimized.

And he succeeded.

Everything he touched, he refined. Every problem, he reduced. Every system, he improved.

Except his own life.

Because while he understood how things worked, he never learned why people stayed. He never built bonds, never nurtured connection. In the quiet halls of his mansion—filled with books, machines, and brilliance—there was no one.

When death finally came, it did not frighten him.

The realization did.

“I understood systems… but I never understood connection.”

That was the only thing he could not solve.<br>And it became the only thing that mattered.



### ⚖️ The Transfer

The entity that found David was not a god of justice, nor a force of balance.

It was something far beyond the system—<br> a Tier-7 existence.

And it was… bored.

Not in the human sense of restlessness, but in the way something that has seen too much begins to lose interest in repetition. Worlds rise, systems stabilize, patterns repeat. Nothing surprises it anymore.

Until David.

Not because David was powerful.<br> Not because he was righteous.

But because he was *interesting*.

A mind that treated reality like code.<br> A man who solved everything—except the one problem that mattered.<br> And more importantly…

A concept.

Something unfinished.<br> Something scalable.<br> Something that might actually behave unpredictably under pressure.

That was enough.

No grand judgment.<br> No divine purpose.

Just a passing thought:

“This could be entertaining.”

So it acted.

Not out of kindness.<br> Not out of cruelty.

Just curiosity.



### 🎁 The Gifts (To Manifest at Age 18)

#### 1. Memory Retention

David does not forget.

Not in the way others do.

His memories persist with clarity—structured, accessible, and interconnected. Knowledge from his past life remains intact, allowing him to think across disciplines, to recognize patterns others would miss, and to approach problems from angles no one else considers.

But this is not power without cost.

He does not gain mastery instantly.<br>He does not bypass effort.<br>He does not succeed automatically.

What he gains is **leverage**:

- the ability to learn faster
- the capacity to attempt complex systems earlier than most
- the foundation to build where others are still trying to understand

He still has to struggle.

He just struggles… more efficiently.



#### 2. The Class — Cognitive Network

This was his final creation.

A concept born on his deathbed, shaped by loneliness, refined by logic, and inspired by the architecture of networks—clients, servers, nodes, and flow.

But with one critical difference.

David is both **client and server**.

He is part of the system he controls.

This is not domination.<br>It is integration.

The class is built on the idea that connection is shared, overlapping, and sometimes messy. Hence there needs to be a layer, a bridge that allows Thoughts, intent, perception, and influence to flow between nodes.

At its core, *Cognitive Network* is not about control.

It is about **connection at scale**.

A system that can grow, adapt, and evolve as more nodes—people, entities, constructs—become linked within it.



### 🧬 Rebirth

David is born again, not into obscurity, but into foundation.

- **Father:** A Tier-2 Battle Mage<br>A man of precision and force, combining combat instincts with spellcraft. From him, David inherits battlefield awareness, tactical thinking, and the ability to apply knowledge under pressure.
- **Mother:** A Tier-2 Alchemist<br>A master of preparation and transformation. From her, David inherits patience, system design, and long-term planning—the understanding that outcomes are built, not improvised.

This is not coincidence.

It is alignment.

From his father, he learns how to act.<br>From his mother, he learns how to prepare.<br>From himself… he must learn how to connect.



### ⚙️ Core Tension

David begins his new life with everything he once valued:

Knowledge.<br>Structure.<br>Potential.

And the one thing he failed to understand:

Connection.

The difference now is simple.

Before, he ignored it.<br>Now, it is the foundation of his power.

And if he fails again—

It won’t just cost him his life.

It will break the system he was chosen to build.
