**Formation:** Every signatory must understand the material terms, knowingly accept the stated consequences, sign with their own mana, and pledge the contractually defined portion of that mana as collateral. A coerced, deceived, incapable, or fundamentally uninformed mana signature cannot establish a valid covenant.

**Mana Judgment:** Each signatory’s mana acts as witness, judge, collateral, and enforcement mechanism. It records what its bearer understood, the obligations knowingly accepted, relevant actions and causal involvement, and knowing attempts to evade, sabotage, or violate the agreement. The covenant reconciles the signatories’ mana records and judges cause-and-effect according to the mutually understood substance of the agreement rather than exploitable wording tricks. It requires no external arbiter.

**Enforcement:** A verified breach triggers only the punishment knowingly accepted at formation. Valid terms may impose pledged-mana forfeiture or transfer, severe mana suppression, pain or physical backlash, temporary weakening or ability restriction, damage to mana circulation, or stronger consequences—including permanent harm—only when explicitly understood and accepted. Enforcement scales through the breacher’s own pledged mana; it is not an ordinary hostile spell attack, so normal tier resistance does not negate the signer’s own accepted mana judgment.

**Conceptual Authority:** Once validly signed, ordinary Tier 0–3 dispelling cannot simply erase the covenant. Knowing tampering or suppression attempts may be judged as breach or evasion when covered by the accepted terms. The covenant ends through fulfillment, a valid agreed release clause, or authority capable of contesting the underlying concept. Knowledgeable Tier 3 beings may fear the consequence of signing it, but the spell creates no automatic fear effect and cannot affect an unsigned being.

**Absolute Boundaries:** Cannot bind non-signatories; secretly add or alter terms; punish beyond knowingly accepted consequences; validate contradictory or impossible obligations; or use another person’s mana as collateral without their valid signature. It grants Seris no general Tier 6 authority and cannot be copied or traditionally learned from observation.
